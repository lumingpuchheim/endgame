\paragraph{The bishop follows a pawn}\label{bishop-follows-pawn}\index{Opposite-coloured bishops!Bishop follows a pawn}
The defending bishop follows one enemy pawn: it stays on the diagonal behind that pawn and watches
the squares in front of it. This limits the stronger side's options to make progress.
Dvoretsky describes this idea as the pawn ``in the crosshair''~\cite{Dvoretsky:2008-endgame-manual}.
