\paragraph{The bishop follows a pawn}\label{bishop-follows-pawn}\index{Opposite-coloured bishops!Bishop follows a pawn}
The bishop follows a pawn means attacking the enemy pawns with the bishop, either to force the pawns
to advance, which helps to blockade them later, or else to tie the king to their defence. The
explanation is Dvoretsky's; he calls the idea ``pawns in the crosshairs''~\cite{Dvoretsky:2008-endgame-manual}.
