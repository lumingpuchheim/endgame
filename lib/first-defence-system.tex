\paragraph{Opposite-coloured bishops: the first defensive system}\label{first-defence-system}\index{Opposite-coloured bishops!First defensive system}
Dvoretsky describes it like this: ``The king blockades the enemy passed pawn while the bishop defends
its own pawns. This is the basic and usually the most secure defensive arrangement. Attempts to break
down the first defensive system involve the creation of a second passed pawn, for example by means
of a pawn breakthrough''~\cite{Dvoretsky:2008-endgame-manual}.\index{Opposite-coloured bishops!Pawn breakthrough}
