\paragraph{En prise}\label{en-prise}\index{En prise}
A piece is \term{en-prise} when the opponent can simply take it: it is attacked and not defended, or
attacked by a piece worth less. Before every move, check which of your pieces are en prise, and which
will be after your move. A move that blocks a check or defends one piece often leaves another one
hanging, and in the endgame a lost piece usually decides the game.
