\paragraph{Blocking on the colour of the opponent's bishop}\label{block-on-bishop-colour}\index{Blocking on the colour of the opponent's bishop}
As soon as the enemy pawns are blocked on squares of the same colour as their own bishop, the draw
becomes obvious.
