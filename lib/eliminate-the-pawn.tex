\paragraph{Eliminate the pawn}\label{eliminate-the-pawn}\index{Eliminate the pawn}

The pawn is often the only advantage the attacker has. If the defender can
\term{eliminate-the-pawn}, the draw is immediate and clear-cut.\index{Practical play!Clear-cut draw} Before anything else, the
defender looks for a way to do that.
