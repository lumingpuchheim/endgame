\paragraph{Switching the defenders}\label{switch-the-defender}\index{Switching the defenders}
Against two passed pawns, the defending king and bishop can swap their jobs. The bishop takes over
the pawn that the king was watching, and the king goes to stop the other pawn. This is useful when
the king is in the wrong place: it can reach the pawn that the bishop alone cannot hold, or a
corner where the position is a theoretical draw.
