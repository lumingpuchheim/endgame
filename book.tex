\chapter{Rook vs Rook}\label{ch:rook-vs-rook}
Rook endings come up in many games, and no other endgame costs strong players as many half
points (\xref{chapter}{ch:where-to-start}).

The positions below are taken from games between grandmasters and other strong players. In each
one, the player to move went wrong. They are ordered from the fewest pawns on the board to the
most, so the first sections hold the cleanest examples.

Before you read the moves, cover them and find the right move yourself. Then read on: the
mistake is marked with ?, and the rest of the game shows what happened. If a later game reaches
a position you have already seen, the book says so and stops there.
