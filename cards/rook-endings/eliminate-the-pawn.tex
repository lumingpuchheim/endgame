\paragraph{Eliminate the pawn}\index{Rook endings!Eliminate the pawn}

The pawn is often the only advantage the attacker has. If the defender can
\vocab[rook endings]{eliminate-the-pawn}{eliminate the pawn}{Win or exchange the attacker's last
pawn. Without it, a rook ending is a draw at once}, the draw is immediate and clear-cut.\index{Practical play!Clear-cut draw} Before anything else, the
defender looks for a way to do that.
