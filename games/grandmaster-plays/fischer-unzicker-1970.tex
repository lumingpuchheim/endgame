\subsection*{My Reflection}

I am happy that I found some right moves with the Lasker--Capablanca game as a reference.
I was not resourceful enough to create a mating threat that would give my opponent a chance to make
a mistake. I am also completely unfamiliar with the resulting knight-versus-bishop ending, and I failed
to realize that White is winning.
