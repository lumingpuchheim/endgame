\chapter{Two bishops vs bishop and knight}\label{ch:two-bishops-vs-bishop-and-knight}
Two bishops vs bishop and knight came up in 1.5 of every 100 games. Once it was on the board, strong players blundered 63 times per 100 games (\xref{chapter}{ch:go-wrong}). It is number 14 among the endgames of this book in the order of study (\xref{chapter}{ch:where-to-start}).

The positions below are taken from games between grandmasters and other strong players. In each one, the player to move went wrong. They are ordered from the fewest pawns on the board to the most, so the first sections hold the cleanest examples.

Before you read the moves, cover them and find the right move yourself. Then read on: the mistake is marked with ?, and the rest of the game shows what happened. If a game reaches a position you have already seen, the book says so and stops there.
